About

I'm a software developer who builds backend systems on C# and SQL Server. I studied Computer Science, where I first went deep into object-oriented programming and relational databases — and that's still where my instincts come from.

When I design a system's architecture, I pay the most attention to two things: keeping business logic cleanly organized, and properly normalizing the database schema. I've seen how a poor database design turns into a performance problem months later — so I'd rather solve it at the design stage.

These days I work on enterprise-level applications, REST APIs and reporting systems, and I keep experimenting with new tools through freelance and personal projects on the side.
